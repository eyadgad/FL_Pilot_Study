# NC-SACPA full-study outcome

Frozen gate outcome: **REVISE_OR_KILL**. The method beat the xFedAlign-style median baseline on primary JSD in all shifted families, but failed functional non-inferiority and the predecessor-mechanism gate. Top-k oracle overlap fell materially, revealing that lower JSD/EDI can coexist with worse feature-support fidelity. The simpler global-mean baseline also achieved very low JSD while degrading top-k/functional behavior, motivating the rank-fidelity pivot.
